MATES Health & Blood Donation

A caring society values the health and wellbeing of everyone. This initiative aims to encourage health awareness, blood grouping and voluntary blood donation in collaboration with qualified and authorised organisations.

Our intended focus

  • Community health awareness
  • Blood grouping
  • Voluntary blood donation awareness
  • Collaboration with authorised providers
