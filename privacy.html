Privacy, accessibility and information about this website.

Your privacy

This website does not provide accounts, accept payments or send form submissions. The contact page prepares a message locally in your browser. Information entered there is not transmitted to the foundation and is not saved by the website.

If you download a prepared message, that file is stored on your device. If you copy it, it is placed on your clipboard. Take care when sharing personal information.

Hosting and cookies

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, as part of providing the service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices. This website does not add analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies.

Program information

Initiatives describe the foundation’s intended areas of focus. Program availability, eligibility, dates, governance, registration details and donation arrangements should be confirmed through official announcements when available.

Accessibility

The site supports keyboard navigation, readable text, responsive layouts and reduced motion preferences. If you encounter a barrier, please report it through an official contact channel once available.

Logo and content

The MATES Foundation logo is used as supplied. Written content introduces the foundation’s purpose and does not claim verified activity or impact beyond the information provided.
