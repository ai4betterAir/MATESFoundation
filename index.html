THE SPIRIT OF MATES

Opportunity to learn.
Support when it matters.
A future we build together.

A better society grows when people have the support to move forward.

MATES Foundation connects people who want to help with the needs that matter in our communities. Our focus spans education, scholarships, community wellbeing, health, emergency assistance and practical skills.

Our starting point is simple: listen with respect, understand the need and work together on a useful response. Every person deserves dignity, and every contribution can have a purpose.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things you might want to know.

Every contribution starts with a conversation.

How can I get involved?

Start with your interests and the time or skills you can offer. Explore the volunteer and partnership options, then prepare an introduction on our contact page.

Can organisations partner with MATES?

Yes. We welcome conversations with educational institutions, community groups, businesses and professionals who share our purpose. Specific partnerships are agreed individually.

Are applications and donations open?

Program applications and online donations are not open yet. Confirmed activities, eligibility and verified donation details will be published on this website.

Can I use this website in Bengali?

Yes. Choose English or বাংলা in the header. The whole website is available in both languages, and your browser remembers your choice.
