About MATES Foundation

People coming together with a shared commitment to a better society.

Who we are

MATES Foundation brings together people who want to contribute their time, knowledge and practical skills to support others. Our work is guided by care for people, respect for dignity and a belief in the value of shared effort.

We aim to connect willing people with community needs through education, support and opportunities to learn and grow.

Our mission

Bring people together to support learning, respond thoughtfully to community needs and help create opportunities.

Our vision

A society where people feel supported, learning is encouraged and everyone has the chance to move forward.

The values we work by

Dignity
Treat people with care and respect.
Inclusion
Welcome people from different backgrounds.
Compassion
Listen carefully and respond thoughtfully.
Responsibility
Make careful commitments and share information clearly.

How we work

Listen to the need

Understand what matters to people and communities.

Work together
